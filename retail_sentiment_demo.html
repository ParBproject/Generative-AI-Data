Retail review sentiment

Sentiment uses DistilBERT SST-2, topics use a small DeBERTa zero-shot model, and the summary uses DistilBART. They download in the browser on the first analysis. Topic scores are separate relevance scores, so more than one theme can be high. The summary is not a chat model. Reviews longer than 2,000 characters are cut before analysis.


Models stay unloaded until you analyze a review.
